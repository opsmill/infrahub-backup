Plakar repositories can now be encrypted at rest, so a leaked or stolen repository is unreadable without its passphrase. Create one with `--encrypt` and supply the passphrase non-interactively through `INFRAHUB_BACKUP_PASSPHRASE` or `--passphrase-file`; every later backup, restore and list against that repository requires it. A wrong or missing passphrase fails before any read or write, so it can never leave partial or corrupt output. Plaintext repositories are unchanged, and `--encrypt-key` is now rejected with `--backend plakar` rather than silently ignored.
