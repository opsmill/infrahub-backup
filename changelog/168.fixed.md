`restore` no longer reports an Enterprise backup as incompatible with a Community Edition deployment when it could not detect the target's Neo4j edition. A failed edition query now stops the restore of an Enterprise backup with an error that gives the reason the query failed, names the `infrahub-server` container when the Neo4j credentials could not be read from it, and says how to supply them. Community Edition backups are still restored when the edition cannot be detected, and Plakar restores of a snapshot without Neo4j data no longer depend on the edition query.
