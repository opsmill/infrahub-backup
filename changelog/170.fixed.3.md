When copying a file into or out of a container fails, the error now includes what kubectl or Docker reported, such as the API server's refusal, instead of only `exit status 1`. This applies to the database log collector of `infrahub-collect` and to the copies `infrahub-backup` makes. On Kubernetes, the error also includes what kubectl reported when scaling a workload down or back up fails, which `infrahub-backup` does during a backup or a restore and `infrahub-collect` does with `--include-backup`. It also includes what kubectl reported when reading the restart counts of a pod's containers fails, so the failure reason of the log collectors and the task worker collector of `infrahub-collect` names the refused verb and resource.
